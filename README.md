# SMCI Research — Reservation-Backed AI Factory

A seven-chapter, independent academic case study of Super Micro Computer, Inc. (NASDAQ: SMCI), addressing **Week 1 (current and proposed models)**, **Week 2 (simulation, sales, and pricing)**, and **Week 3 (operations, quality control, and investment analysis)**.

**Live website:** https://awkwardpancakes.github.io/SMCI-Business-Model/

## Site chapters

1. [Introduction](introduction.html) — current order-to-cash process, financial metrics, and operational shortcomings.
2. [Proposed Business Model](proposed-business-model.html) — the Reservation-Backed AI Factory and pro forma statements.
3. [Business Process Analysis](business-process-analysis.html) — simulation, staffing, customer acquisition, sales algorithms, and probability of success.
4. [Pricing Analysis](pricing-analysis.html) — domestic causal loops, megawatt pricing, customer value, and foreign stock-and-flow modeling.
5. [Operation Analysis](operation-analysis.html) — suppliers, manufacturing, inventory, shipping, distribution, operating efficiency, and a Five-Year Plan with full data tables.
6. [Decision Analysis](decision-analysis.html) — Total Quality Control, operating trends, probability-weighted investment decision tree, and eleven management recommendations.
7. [References](references.html) — 31 cumulative APA-style references for Weeks 1–3.

**Menu headings retain the assignment's required spelling, including "Operation Analysis."**

## Deployment

The repository's GitHub Actions workflow (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) deploys all website files on pushes to the `main` branch. Repository Settings → Pages should use **GitHub Actions** as its source.

## Academic scope and limitations

- The research, model parameters, probability estimates, forecast scenarios, and graphics were supplied for an academic assignment. Forecasts are not verified predictions or official guidance by Super Micro Computer, Inc.
- Exported image files do not provide access to the original Power BI workspace or PBIX files. Any shared Power BI report and Excel calculations are separate course deliverables.
- The site is an independent academic study, not an official Supermicro publication.
